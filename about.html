Practical help from someone who has run the process.

AC Manufacturing Solutions was set up to help small and mid-size manufacturers stabilize their processes and improve throughput without vague improvement programs or long consulting contracts.

How we work

The work starts where the product is made. Recommendations come after observation, not before.

  • Look before recommending

    We watch how a process actually runs, across operators and shifts, and put numbers on what we see before suggesting a change.

  • Document only what removes ambiguity

    Good standards focus on the steps that affect quality and throughput and the decisions that have to be made the same way every time. The rest is clutter.

  • Leave the team able to run it

    Every engagement ends with standards, documents and a plan your people can maintain without us.

  • Scope it, then finish it

    Work is agreed up front with clear deliverables and an end date. No open-ended retainers by default.

Who does the work

AC is led by a Certified Engineering Technologist whose career has run from the inspection bench to launching new products into production.
