Book an intro call.

The first call is for finding the real constraint, checking fit, and deciding whether a scoped engagement makes sense. It isn't a sales pitch.

What we'll cover in 15 to 30 minutes

  • Your process and what good looks like: throughput, scrap, rework, lead time
  • Where instability or variation is showing up
  • What is most likely limiting output
  • Which engagement or package is the right starting point, if any
Email to book a call Or use the contact form

How booking works

Email a short summary and a few times that suit you. We'll reply to confirm a slot.

hello@acmss.ca

Useful to include

  • Process or product family
  • What hurts: scrap, rework, delays, variation, capacity
  • Any operation you suspect is the constraint
  • The outcome you want and by when

What happens next

No forms to fill in beyond a few lines, and no sales sequence afterwards.

  1. You write

    A few lines on the process and the problem, by email or the contact form.

  2. We reply

    Usually within two business days, with times for a call or a straight answer if we're not the right fit.

  3. The call

    15 to 30 minutes on where the time and the scrap are going. You keep whatever we spot.

  4. The scope

    If it's a fit, a short written scope with deliverables, a fixed price and an end date.
