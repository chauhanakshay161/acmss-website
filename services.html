Primary engagements

Process standardization and throughput stabilization

Start here

For manufacturing and operations leaders facing inconsistent execution, tribal knowledge risk, throughput variability, or dependence on a few key people.

The problem

Processes are not repeatable or clearly defined, which limits throughput, onboarding and any improvement effort built on top of them.

What it does

  • Finds the process constraints and where variation comes from
  • Turns undocumented knowledge into standards people use
  • Sets a stable baseline for future improvement

Typical deliverables

  • Targeted SOPs, setup sheets and work instructions
  • Supporting CAD where a drawing removes ambiguity
  • Process flow summaries
  • Stability and throughput assessment for management
  • Prioritized improvement roadmap

What it is not

  • Generic Lean training
  • Documenting every operation in the building
  • Open-ended hourly support

Manufacturing readiness and process industrialization

For teams preparing to launch a new product, scale production, or transfer work between lines, plants or suppliers.

The problem

The product or process is technically complete but not ready for repeatable, predictable production.

What it does

  • Assesses production readiness from the shop floor's point of view
  • Finds gaps between design intent and what the floor can hold
  • Reduces risk before volume or transfer decisions are made

Typical deliverables

  • Manufacturing readiness assessment
  • Process definition and standardization
  • Critical tooling or part CAD where needed
  • Work instructions for early or unstable operations
  • Risk register with mitigation actions

What it is not

  • Product design ownership
  • NPI program management
  • Prototype iteration

Throughput, capacity and bottleneck analysis

For managers who suspect a hidden constraint, are missing output targets, or are weighing overtime, outsourcing or capital spend.

The problem

Throughput is limited by constraints nobody fully understands, rather than by true capacity.

What it does

  • Identifies the real bottleneck and what drives its variation
  • Separates perception from observation and data
  • Shows where improvement or investment will actually matter

Typical deliverables

  • Constraint and bottleneck analysis
  • Cycle time and variability assessment
  • Capacity confidence summary
  • Short and medium term improvement options
  • Decision summary for leadership

What it is not

  • Time studies for their own sake
  • ERP reconfiguration
  • A continuous improvement to-do list

Specialist engagements

Documentation and knowledge capture

For organizations with retirement risk, undocumented methods, or audit, quality and onboarding problems.

The problem

Critical manufacturing knowledge lives in people, not in systems.

What it does

  • Captures and formalizes critical process knowledge
  • Produces documents that reflect how work is really done
  • Reduces risk tied to a few key people

Typical deliverables

  • SOPs and work instructions for critical processes
  • Supporting CAD or sketches where clarity is needed
  • Standardization plan aligned to actual execution
  • Document structure and control guidance

What it is not

  • General writing services
  • Cosmetic clean-up of existing documents
  • Checkbox documentation for an audit

Automation and capital decision support

For leaders considering automation, new equipment or a major process investment.

The problem

Capital decisions are being made without a stable process baseline or clear payback logic.

What it does

  • Assesses whether the process is ready to automate
  • Identifies prerequisites that are often missed
  • Reduces the risk of premature or misdirected spend

Typical deliverables

  • Automation readiness assessment
  • Process stability gap analysis
  • High-level feasibility and risk framing
  • What to stabilize before investing

What it is not

  • Machine specification or vendor selection
  • Controls or robot programming
  • Turnkey automation projects
