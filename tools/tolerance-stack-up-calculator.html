    Worst case or RSS?

    Both answer "will it fit?", with different assumptions about how parts vary.

    Worst case
    Adds every tolerance at its limit. If worst case passes, every assembly fits, guaranteed. It is the safe choice for small quantities, safety-critical fits and processes you don't control.
    RSS
    Root sum of squares: the square root of the sum of each half-tolerance squared. It predicts the range most assemblies fall in, because parts rarely sit at their limits all at once.
    When RSS is valid
    When each dimension varies independently, is roughly normal and centred, and its process is capable, with the tolerance equal to about ±3 standard deviations. Check capability with the Cpk calculator.
    Contribution
    Each tolerance's share of the RSS variance, its half-tolerance squared over the total. Tighten the biggest contributor first; tightening a small one changes almost nothing.
    Limits
    This is a one-dimensional stack. Geometric tolerances such as position, profile and perpendicularity, and 2D or 3D effects, need a fuller analysis.
