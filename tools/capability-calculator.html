    How to read the numbers

    Capability compares the spread of your process with the width of the tolerance. The four indices answer slightly different questions.

    Cp
    Could the process fit inside the tolerance if it were perfectly centred? It uses short-term variation and ignores where the mean sits.
    Cpk
    Does it fit where it actually runs? The distance from the mean to the nearer limit, divided by three short-term sigma. This is the number most customers ask for.
    Pp and Ppk
    The same questions using overall variation, which includes drift between setups, shifts and material lots. A big gap between Cpk and Ppk means the process isn't stable over time.
    What is a good Cpk?
    Below 1.00 the process is not capable. 1.00 to 1.33 is marginal. 1.33 is the common minimum for production, and 1.67 is typical for new or safety-critical characteristics.
    Short-term sigma
    Estimated from the average moving range for individual readings, or the average subgroup range for subgroups of 2 to 10. Overall sigma is the ordinary sample standard deviation.
    Before you trust it
    Cpk assumes the process is stable, the data are roughly normal and the gauge is good enough. A capable-looking number from an unstable process or a poor gauge is not real.
