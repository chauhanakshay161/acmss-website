Illustration: measurements of a bore diameter with a tolerance of plus or minus 0.020 mm. With each operator using their own method, readings spread wide, the process capability index is 0.37 and about 124 parts in 1,000 are out of tolerance. After the process is measured and standardized, the spread narrows to 0.024 mm and the capability index reaches 1.67.

Most throughput problems are not speed problems. They are stability problems, and stability is something you can measure.

  • Output depends on who is on shift

    Setups vary by operator, methods drift, and results vary with them.

  • Critical steps live in one person's head

    When that person is away or retires, first-pass yield drops and nobody can say exactly why.

  • Nobody is sure where the constraint is

    Capacity feels tight, but the real bottleneck has never been measured, or it keeps moving.
